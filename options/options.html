Dev Toolbox

Jump between environments of the same app without losing your place.

Projects

Group the domains one app runs on. On any of them, right-click or use the toolbar icon to open the same page in another environment. Subdomains carry over (app.test.com → app.test-qa.com), and ports work too (localhost:3000).
